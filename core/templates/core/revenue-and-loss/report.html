Calculation method and limitations

  • Revenue is the sum of sales-order line totals in USD (merchandise only); tax and freight are excluded.
  • Cost of goods is quantity sold × the standard cost in effect on the order date from ProductCostHistory, falling back to the product's current cost when no history row covers that date.
  • Gross profit = revenue − cost of goods; gross margin = gross profit ÷ revenue.
  • Below-cost means a USD line total below quantity × standard cost on the order date; the shortfall is the negative financial result shown.
  • Invoiced total is the order header's total due in USD, including tax and freight.
  • Net profit and accounting loss cannot be calculated: the database has no operating expenses (payroll, rent, depreciation, interest). No figures are estimated.
